Welcome to the Mental Health Wellness App

Our app is designed to support your journey to better mental health through cutting-edge technology and personalized tools.

  • 🌟 AI-Powered Wellness Plans: Our intelligent system analyzes your mood and journal entries to create customized mental health tips and action plans tailored just for you.
  • 📈 Mood Tracking: Track your emotional patterns over time and identify trends with easy-to-use graphs and daily inputs.
  • 🧘 Personalized Goals: Set mental wellness goals and monitor your progress every day.
  • 🗓️ Therapy Session Scheduling: Easily schedule virtual therapy appointments with reminders so you never miss a session.
  • 🆘 Emergency Support: Quickly access your emergency contacts if you're in distress, right from the dashboard.

Our mission is to combine proactive self-care with professional support to help you take control of your mental wellness journey.
